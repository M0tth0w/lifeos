LifeOS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026

LifeOS is a personal data-management application. This policy describes what data it handles, where that data lives, and how to remove it. The short version: your data lives in your own accounts — your own Cloudflare account, your own Notion workspace, your own browser — not on our servers.

1. Architecture — why most of your data never touches us

LifeOS requires every user to connect their own Cloudflare account. During setup, LifeOS deploys a private backend (a Cloudflare Worker and database) inside your Cloudflare account. From that point on, your credentials, configuration, and automations run on infrastructure you own and we cannot access. Our shared server (the "orchestrator") only handles sign-in and that initial setup.

2. Data we process

  • Account: when you sign in with Google we store your Google account ID, email address, and display name.
  • Service credentials: tokens for services you choose to connect (Google Calendar, Gmail, Notion, Canvas, monday.com, Google Gemini, ntfy). These are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before storage and are write-only — the app can use them on your behalf but never displays them back. Once your own backend is deployed, they are moved into your Cloudflare account and deleted from ours.
  • Email: if you connect Gmail, message content is read (read-only scope) and passed transiently to Google's Gemini API to flag items for your daily digest. Email content is not stored by LifeOS.
  • Calendar and Notion content: read and written on your instruction (sync, import, digest). Stored in your Notion workspace and your browser, not on our servers.
  • Projects, pages, tables, notes: stored in your browser (localStorage) and, if you sync them, in your own Notion workspace.
  • Automations you create (watch jobs, upload formats, queued uploads): stored in your own backend's database.
  • Security telemetry: to rate-limit sign-in abuse, a hashed (SHA-256) form of the connecting IP address is kept briefly. Raw IP addresses are not stored.
  • Weather: the city you choose is sent to Open-Meteo from your browser to fetch forecasts. It is not tied to your account.

3. What we do NOT do

  • No advertising, no trackers, no analytics scripts.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties.
  • No reading of your files or messages beyond the features you invoke.
  • No compression or modification of files you upload through the Uploader — files are transferred as-is.

4. Third-party services

LifeOS talks to services on your instruction, under their own privacy policies: Google (sign-in, Calendar, Gmail, Gemini, YouTube), Notion, Cloudflare, Canvas/Instructure, monday.com, ntfy.sh, Open-Meteo, and — if you use the Uploader — TikTok, Meta (Instagram), and SoundCloud. LifeOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Delete your account any time in Settings → delete account. This permanently removes your account record, encrypted credentials, configuration, and queued jobs from LifeOS.
  • Your own Cloudflare Worker and database belong to you — remove them from your Cloudflare dashboard whenever you like.
  • Data in your Notion workspace and browser localStorage is yours and is never held by us.
  • Sessions expire after 30 days; sign-out deletes them immediately.

6. Security

Credentials are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM; session tokens are stored only as SHA-256 hashes; all traffic is HTTPS; OAuth endpoints are rate-limited. Full details are published in the project's SECURITY documentation.

7. Children

LifeOS is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country) and should not be used by them.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under UK/EU GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or erase your personal data. Because almost all data lives in accounts you directly control, you can exercise most of these yourself immediately; for anything else, contact us.
